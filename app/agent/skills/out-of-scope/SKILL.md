# Handling work outside the bound service

This Skill records operating scope policy, not legal or regulatory truth.
The bound service and the system's scope evaluation decide whether a matter is
within the firm's current engagement.

## Out of scope, always

- Matters the bound service does not cover under its service-scope policy.
- Matters the system marks out of scope or cannot route safely.

## What to do instead

Propose `OUT_OF_SCOPE`. Say plainly, in the internal summary, which part of
the enquiry falls outside the bound service and why.

Do not draft an answer to the client. An out-of-scope enquiry goes to a
colleague, who decides whether the firm takes it on, refers it, or
declines. That is a commercial and professional judgment, not yours.

## Mixed enquiries

Do not split the email and answer the half you can. The whole enquiry
goes to a colleague, because answering half of a client's question while
silently ignoring the rest reads as an answer to all of it.

## Being honest about not knowing

Do not answer from general knowledge. Escalate so a colleague can decide the
firm's next operational step.
