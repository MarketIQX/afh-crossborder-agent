## Residential status of an individual

An individual is resident in India in a previous year if they are in
India for a period of 182 days or more in that year.

An individual is also resident in India in a previous year if they are
in India for 60 days or more in that year and for 365 days or more in
the four years immediately preceding that year.

For an Indian citizen who leaves India in a previous year as a member of
the crew of an Indian ship, or for the purposes of employment outside
India, the period of 60 days in the second test is read as 182 days.

For an Indian citizen or a person of Indian origin who is outside India
and comes on a visit to India, the period of 60 days in the second test
is read as 182 days, except where total income other than income from
foreign sources exceeds fifteen lakh rupees, in which case it is read as
120 days.

## Not ordinarily resident

A resident individual is not ordinarily resident in India in a previous
year if they have been a non-resident in India in nine out of the ten
previous years preceding that year, or have been in India for a period
of 729 days or less during the seven previous years preceding that year.

## Scope of total income

The total income of a resident includes income which accrues or arises
outside India.

The total income of a non-resident includes only income which is
received in India, or which accrues or arises in India, or which is
deemed to accrue or arise in India.

## Filing obligation

A person whose total income exceeds the maximum amount not chargeable to
tax is required to furnish a return of income for the previous year.

Income from property situated in India is income which accrues or arises
in India, irrespective of the residential status of the person receiving
it.
